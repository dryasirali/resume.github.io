Research profile

Secure and resilient cyber-physical systems

Bridging control theory, physical-layer security, coding theory, and intelligent autonomous systems.

My research focuses on secure and resilient cyber-physical systems, with emphasis on communication security, resilient control, networked control systems, Polar Codes, UAV systems, chaotic encryption, and AI/ML-enabled optimization. My work bridges control theory, physical-layer security, coding theory, and intelligent autonomous systems, with publications in leading journals including IEEE Transactions and Elsevier journals.
